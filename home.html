Welcome

One place for room status, announcements, assignments, group work , and attendance.

Install UR-CAFF UPDATE on your phone

Opens full-screen like a real app — no browser bar, no typing the address again.

Install on iPhone/iPad

  1. Tap the Share icon at the bottom of Safari (a square with an arrow pointing up)
  2. Scroll down the menu and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  3. Tap "Add" in the top right corner
What you can do here
Rooms

Room status

See which rooms are free or occupied right now, before you walk over. Updated live by your CP/CPN.
Announcements

Announcements

Messages from your CP/CPN and staff, you only see the ones posted for your year and department.
Assignments

Assignments

Submit your work with a link to your file. See who's finished, or your group's live progress.
Groups

Groups

See which group you're in and who else is on it. Used for shared group assignments.
Attendance

Attendance

Show your check-in code to your CP/CPN or staff to be marked present or start a session and scan students in.

How it works

  1. Everyone signs in with their own account students, CP/CPN, and staff.
  2. What you see is scoped to your year and department, so it stays relevant.
  3. CP/CPN keep room status and announcements up to date for their group.
  4. Staff can manage roles for the whole platform from the Admin page.
